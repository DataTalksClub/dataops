## Computation lives in one place: backend/src/finance/summary.ts

- `backend/src/finance/summary.ts:19` — `PeriodQuery` is
  `{ month } | { year } | { ytd: true }`; `:125-139` — `resolvePeriod` builds
  the period for each kind: month = `from 01` to `monthEnd`, months `[month]`;
  year = `YYYY-01-01` to `YYYY-12-31` with all 12 months; YTD = `YYYY-01-01`
  to today with months up to the current one, label `<year> YTD (to <today>)`.
- `:66-88` — `Totals` carries `moneyIn`, `moneyOut`, `net` (plus net profit
  excluding VAT, VAT lines, taxes, TK, owner pay); `:303-356` — `total()` sums
  per entry in integer cents and derives `net = moneyIn - moneyOut` (`:346`).
  Income rows count as money in, expense rows as money out (`:324`); supplier
  credit notes are contra-expense, neither in nor out (`:313-323`).
- `:433-520` — `summarize` filters rows into the period by booking date and
  returns per-month totals (`:465-469` — every month of the period with its own
  `Totals`, future months flagged), `byCurrency` (`:471-514`) and gaps.
- Rules are documented in `SOURCE_RULES` (`:106-120`) and
  `docs/finance-summary.md`; both are returned/rendered as `rules`.
- `backend/src/finance/service.ts:84` — `financeSummary` loads the ledger,
  statements, coverage and ECB rates and calls `summarize`;
  `:130` — `parsePeriodQuery` validates exactly one of `month=`, `year=`,
  `ytd=1`.
- `backend/src/routes/finance.ts:10,17-22` — `GET /api/finance/summary?month=|year=|ytd=1`
  serves it (400 on a bad query, 405 on non-GET).
