# Social Post Type Taxonomy

Derived from analysis of 216 historical Typefully posts for DataTalks.Club Zoomcamps. Each post draft must define its `primary_type`, `course_phase`, `audience`, `angle`, `proof_asset`, `cta`, and optional `urgency`.

## 13 Primary Post Types

1. **Course Launch Announcement:** Main kick-off post declaring the cohort open for registration with start date, format, and core promise.
2. **Syllabus / Curriculum Walkthrough:** Structured breakdown of modules, topics, and tools covered week by week.
3. **Prerequisite & Audience Explainer:** Answering "Who is this course for?" and clarifying expected background (e.g. basic Python/SQL).
4. **Educational Micro-Lesson:** Teaching one concrete idea or solution directly in the post (with code or diagram).
5. **Tool Landscape / Stack Explainer:** Highlighting the specific tools taught and explaining why they are used in production.
6. **Alumni Story / Career Transition:** Highlighting a real graduate who transitioned into a data or ML engineering role.
7. **Student Project Showcase:** Highlighting an impressive midterm or capstone project built during a previous cohort.
8. **Live Event Announcement:** Promoting the pre-course workshop, Q&A webinar, or live launch stream.
9. **FAQ & Objection Buster:** Answering common doubts regarding time commitment, free pricing, or certificate eligibility.
10. **Learning in Public Prompt:** Encouraging learners to share their weekly notes, commits, or homework progress on LinkedIn/X.
11. **Weekly Module / Homework Rhythm:** Announcing module releases on Mondays and homework reminders on Fridays.
12. **Project Milestone Kickoff:** Announcing midterm or capstone project requirements and peer review guidelines.
13. **Cohort Completion & Results:** Celebrating graduate numbers, certificates issued, and sharing the project gallery.

## 5 Modifier Categories

- **Urgency:** `starts-today`, `starts-next-week`, `deadline-tonight`, `last-chance`.
- **Proof Asset:** `testimonial`, `github-stars`, `student-project-link`, `metrics`.
- **Event Link:** `luma-registration`, `youtube-live-stream`.
- **Resource Link:** `github-repo`, `website-article`, `documentation`.
- **Community:** `slack-channel`, `telegram-group`.
