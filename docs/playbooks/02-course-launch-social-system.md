# Course Launch Social System

This playbook defines how to run multi-channel social and email campaigns for DataTalks.Club Zoomcamp launches. It links the high-level strategy to the day-to-day drafting in Typefully.

## Core Principles

1. **One Job Per Post:** Every post has exactly one primary objective: demand creation, education, course positioning, proof, event, community, resource, project, partner, or urgency.
2. **Proof Before Urgency:** Never rely on artificial FOMO or countdowns early in the cycle. Establish the curriculum quality, alumni outcomes, and tool relevance first.
3. **Channel-Specific Tone:**
   - **Alexey LinkedIn:** Practitioner-to-practitioner lessons, engineering insights, course philosophy, authentic reflections.
   - **DTC LinkedIn:** Official announcements, syllabus guides, event dates, student project showcases, community milestones.
   - **X / Twitter:** Punchy hooks, bite-sized code snippets, architecture diagrams, live event reminders.
   - **DTC Newsletter:** Deep weekly roundup, sponsored feature, Book of the Week, and primary course CTA.
   - **Reddit:** Genuinely written for the specific subreddit community. Strictly 2 posts per cohort (T-30 days and T+0).

## Post Categories

### 1. Demand Creation
- **Problem framing:** Name the painful skill gap the Zoomcamp solves.
- **Market / context:** Why this skill matters right now (e.g. LLMs in production, modern data stacks).
- **Instructor thesis:** Why we teach this topic in a project-driven way.
- **Audience segmentation:** Who the course is for, and who should skip it.

### 2. Educational Content
- **Micro-lessons:** Teach one concrete concept from an upcoming module with a code sample or diagram.
- **Tool landscape:** Explain the exact stack (e.g. Docker, Terraform, Kestra, dbt, Spark, Qdrant).
- **Common pitfalls:** "Top 3 mistakes engineers make when building RAG systems."

### 3. Proof & Community
- **Alumni stories:** Real career transitions and project wins.
- **Project highlights:** Video demos or GitHub repos from past students.
- **Milestone stats:** "Over 10,000 learners registered across 5 cohorts."

### 4. Logistics & Urgency
- **Syllabus walkthrough:** Module-by-module breakdown.
- **Live Q&A reminder:** "Ask Alexey anything live this Tuesday at 17:00 Berlin time."
- **Starts today:** Final CTA on launch Monday with direct registration links.

## Drafting & Approval Workflow

1. **Drafting:** Valeriia drafts posts in Typefully following the post taxonomy and assigns them to the appropriate channel schedule.
2. **Review:** Alexey reviews drafts for personal accounts (Alexey LinkedIn / X).
3. **Scheduling:** Scheduled via Typefully to post during peak engagement hours (08:00–10:00 CET and 14:00–16:00 CET on weekdays).
4. **Follow-Up:** Valeriia and Grace monitor comments, answer prerequisite questions, and feed repeated objections into `faq.md`.
